About Me

Academic Profile and Professional Biography

Dr. Getnet Tesfaw Demsie is an Assistant Professor of Social Psychology at Wollo University, Ethiopia. He holds a PhD in Social Psychology from Addis Ababa University.

His doctoral research examined the behavioural outcomes of children left behind due to parental migration in Southern Wollo, Ethiopia.

His academic and research work focuses on the psychosocial experiences of children and youth affected by armed conflict, forced displacement and migration. He is particularly interested in psychosocial well-being, social relationships, social inclusion, childhood adversity and peacebuilding.

He has experience in qualitative and mixed-methods research, participatory approaches, arts-based research, academic teaching and community-engaged research.
